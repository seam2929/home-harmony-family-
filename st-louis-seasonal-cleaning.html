LOCAL GUIDE · ST. LOUIS, MISSOURI

A seasonal cleaning guide for St. Louis homes

A few small routine changes can make it easier to keep up with a home as the weather shifts through the year.

⌂Home Harmony Family LLC
Home care notes for the St. Louis area

St. Louis has four distinct seasons. The National Weather Service describes summers as warm and humid, while spring is normally the wettest season. Those local patterns can shape how dirt, moisture, and leaves find their way indoors. Here are a few simple ways to adjust your cleaning routine through the year.

Local climate details are based on the National Weather Service St. Louis climatology.

01 · SPRING

Make a plan for rainy days.

Spring is typically St. Louis’s wettest season. Keep a washable mat near the entrance, shake it out regularly, and wipe window sills and entry floors as needed. A small basket for wet shoes can also help keep tracked-in dirt in one place.

02 · SUMMER

Stay ahead of everyday buildup.

Warm, humid weather can make busy rooms feel stuffy. Focus on high-touch surfaces, kitchen counters, bathroom sinks, and floors. Let damp towels and bath mats dry fully between uses, and follow the care instructions for your home’s surfaces.

03 · FALL

Give entryways extra attention.

Leaves and outdoor debris can travel inside on shoes. Vacuum or sweep the entry more often, clean around door tracks, and wash floor mats when they need it. A quick reset at the doorway can make the rest of the home easier to maintain.

04 · WINTER

Keep a simple indoor routine.

On wet or snowy days, use an absorbent entrance mat and wipe up tracked-in moisture promptly. Wash throws and bedding on their normal schedule, and choose a short weekly reset that feels manageable for your household.

A local note about trash and recycling

Inside the City of St. Louis, residents can use the City’s address search to look up refuse, recycling, bulky-item, and yard-waste collection days. Nearby municipalities may use different schedules, so check the local rules for your own address.
